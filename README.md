# Investigation of Potable Water Quality Parameters in Shyamnagar, Bangladesh

**Funded by:** University Grants Commission (UGC), Bangladesh — Student Research Project (2026)
**Principal Investigator:** Md. Osman Goni, M.Sc. Student, University of Barishal
**Supervisor:** Dr. Hafiz Ashraful Haque, Associate Professor, University of Barishal

## Overview

This project assesses drinking-water quality and associated health and behavioral risks in climate-vulnerable **Shyamnagar Upazila, Satkhira**. The study focuses on salinity intrusion, physicochemical properties, heavy-metal contamination, water potability, and community adaptation.

## Key Objectives

* Assess physicochemical properties of drinking water.
* Measure selected heavy metals and essential minerals.
* Evaluate potability using **Water Quality Index (WQI)** and drinking-water standards.
* Examine health risks and behavioral responses to water contamination.

## Methodology

Water samples will be collected from tube wells, pond sand filters, and surface-water sources using GPS-tagged sampling sites. Laboratory analysis will follow **APHA Standard Methods**, covering pH, EC, TDS, salinity, DO, turbidity, major ions, and selected heavy metals (As, Pb, Cd, Cr, Fe, Mn).

WQI, statistical analysis in **R/SPSS**, and GIS-based spatial interpolation in **ArcGIS** will be used to identify water-quality conditions and contamination hotspots.

## Expected Outcomes

* Drinking-water quality and WQI assessment across Shyamnagar.
* Identification of contamination hotspots and vulnerable areas.
* Assessment of health risks and behavioral adaptations.
* Comparison between saline-affected and freshwater-available communities.
